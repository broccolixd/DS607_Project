# AI Usage Disclosure

## 1. Overview

Generative AI tools were used as supplementary resources throughout the development of this project. Their use was primarily to support coding, debugging, research direction, statistical methodology, interpretation of findings and project presentation.

The AI tools used include:
- ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- Google Gemini
- Claude (Anthropic)

AI-generated suggestions and explanations were reviewed and evaluated before being incorporated into the project. The analysis, statistical decisions and final conclusions were checked against the dataset, implemented code, and statistical results.

## 2. Description of AI Usage

| AI Tool | Purpose | Description of Usage |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Code debugging | Used to identify coding errors, troubleshoot Python issues, and suggest solutions when encountering problems during data processing, statistical analysis, and visualisation. |
| Google Gemini | Code debugging | Used as an additional resource to troubleshoot coding errors and explore alternative solutions to implementation issues. |
| Claude | Research direction and methodology | Used to explore ways to address the research questions, identify potentially relevant statistical methods, and consider additional approaches to deepen the analysis. |
| ChatGPT | Documentation | Used to assist in drafting and structuring the `README.md` file, including the project overview, research questions, data description, methodology, findings, and limitations. |
| ChatGPT and Claude | Presentation structure | Used to improve the organisation and flow of the project presentation, including how to communicate the research questions, methodology, statistical findings, and conclusions more clearly. |
| Claude | Interpretation of findings | Used to review the interpretation of statistical results and assess whether the explanations of confidence intervals, hypothesis tests, and other findings were statistically appropriate and consistent with the research questions. |

## 3. Verification and Responsible Use

AI tools were used as supplementary assistance rather than as substitutes for independent analysis and critical evaluation.

The following practices were followed:

- Suggested code was reviewed and tested before being incorporated into the project.
- Statistical methods were evaluated based on their suitability for the research questions and the characteristics of the dataset.
- Statistical outputs were generated from the actual dataset rather than relying on AI-generated numerical results.
- Interpretations suggested by AI tools were checked against the computed results and relevant statistical concepts.
- The final presentation and written documentation were reviewed to ensure consistency with the actual analysis.

## 4. Acknowledgement

The use of ChatGPT, Google Gemini and Claude helped support the technical development, statistical exploration, interpretation and communication of this project.

Responsibility for the final analysis, methodological choices, interpretation and conclusions remains with the project members.
